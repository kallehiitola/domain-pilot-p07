How the calculation works

Multiply the inside length, width, and height of your chosen box to find interior volume, then multiply by your realistic usable fill fraction (accounting for packing paper and headspace). Apply your spare-volume buffer to your boxable inventory volume, divide by usable box capacity, and round up to whole boxes.

What this estimate does not cover

Large furniture, mattresses, and unboxed items are excluded. Heavy items such as books, dishes, and tools reach safe weight limits (40 to 50 lb) long before filling large boxes—always mix small, medium, and large boxes rather than packing everything in one size.
