How to how many moving boxes do i need

Estimate boxes from your measured packed-item volume and a chosen box’s inside dimensions. This makes the assumptions visible instead of promising a fixed number of boxes per bedroom.

Quick answer: 1,000 L of inventory, 45 × 35 × 30 cm boxes, 80% usable fill and 10% spare volume give 30 boxes.

What you need to measure

  • Estimated boxable inventory volume
  • Inside box length
  • Inside box width
  • Inside box height
  • Usable fill fraction
  • Spare volume allowance

How to calculate it

Multiply the inside length, width and height in centimetres and divide by 1,000 for box volume in litres. Multiply by usable fill fraction. Add your spare allowance to the inventory volume, divide by usable box capacity and round up.

What changes the estimate

Estimate inventory volume from existing bins, shelf sections or measured bundles. Large furniture is excluded. Heavy books, fragile items and awkward objects can require more boxes than volume alone suggests; respect each box’s weight rating. Usable fill is an estimate, not a packing guarantee.

Worked example

1,000 L of inventory, 45 × 35 × 30 cm boxes, 80% usable fill and 10% spare volume give 30 boxes.
